## 4. canada_proclamations — CONFIRMED 2026-09-08
**Date/time:** Signed **September 8, 2026** (evening, per Mondaq citing White House); import bans effective **12:01 a.m. ET, Sept 29, 2026**. **Confidence: confirmed.**

**What happened:** Trump issued five presidential proclamations (Nos. 11061–11065) under Section 338 of the Tariff Act of 1930. Three impose outright **import bans** on certain Canadian goods: (i) dairy products, molasses, non-alcoholic beer; (ii) alcoholic beverages (beer, cider, hard liquors, wines); (iii) motorcycles — effective Sept 29. Two others **modify Section 338 duties** (added 50% duties on Canadian cheeses, motorboats, further wood/paper/furniture effective Sept 15; removed rock salt and cement). The same day, Trump directed the GSA to remove ~$50B of Canadian-origin products from its Multiple Award Schedules. All actions landed the same day Canada's retaliatory tariffs on ~$27.6B of US goods (50%/25%/15% rates) took effect.
- First Tier-1-adjacent reporting: White House announced the 8th; covered Sept 8–9 (e.g., Seoul Economic Daily, AP/Yonhap-linked). Sources: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/09/14/2026-18835/excluding-certain-canadian-products-from-importation-into-the-united-states-in-response-to-continued ; https://en.sedaily.com/international/2026/09/09/trump-bans-some-canadian-imports-rallies-base-at-party ; https://www.mondaq.com/canada/export-controls-trade-investment-sanctions/1845160/from-duties-to-import-bans-the-canadaus-tariff-war-after-the-collapse-of-negotiations

## 5. perim_island_seized — CONFIRMED 2026-09-11
**Date/time:** **Friday, September 11, 2026** (government forces withdrew Sept 10). **Confidence: confirmed.**

**What happened:** Yemen's Houthi movement seized **Perim Island (Mayyun)** in the Bab el-Mandeb Strait after Saudi-backed Yemeni government forces withdrew on Sept 10. This capped an offensive that had taken the port city of Mocha (Sept 10), the mainland town of Dhubab opposite Perim, and the Hanish islands — giving the Iran-aligned group effective control of the Yemeni side of Bab el-Mandeb.

## 6. hormuz_strike_pipeline_shutdown — CONFIRMED (strike 2026-09-10; shutdown confirmed 2026-09-11)
**Dates:** Drone strikes on the **morning of Thursday, Sept 10, 2026** (Saudi Energy Ministry statement); **pipeline shutdown confirmed Sept 11** as a "precautionary measure." Working collector date of 09-13 is slightly late — Tier-1 coverage of the strikes/closure began Sept 10–11 (AP said the pipeline was "closed on Friday" = Sept 11). **Confidence: confirmed on dates; note the 09-13 working date.**

**What happened:** Drones struck pump stations of Saudi Arabia's **East-West (Petroline) crude pipeline** in the Riyadh and Medina regions, causing fires, damage, and injuries. Saudi Arabia shut the 1,200 km, ~4–7 mbpd (useful capacity ~5–6 mbpd) pipeline — its main Hormuz-bypass export route to the Red Sea — and blamed **drones launched by Iranian-backed militias in Iraq**; on Sept 13 Iraq dismissed a military commander in Maysan Governorate after investigations confirmed the drones were launched from there. Two regional officials told AP repairs could take **3–5 weeks**; Brent traded above $105/bbl. Two US officials told CNN pump stations adjacent to the line were struck; Sentinel-3 satellite imagery showed a black smoke plume.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_East%E2%80%93West_Crude_Oil_Pipeline_attack ; https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/markets/commodities/what-saudi-arabias-east-west-pipeline-closure-means-for-global-energy-markets/article71466517.ece ; https://cdm.press/news/middle-east/2026/09/22/saudi-arabia-restarts-east-west-oil-pipeline-after-houthi-strikes-yanbu-exports-set-to-resume-tuesday/
